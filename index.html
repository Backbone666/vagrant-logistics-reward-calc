      Frequently Asked Questions

      What are the collateral limits for courier contracts?

      Standard subcapital collateral limit is 5 Billion ISK across all Blockade Runner, Deep Space Transport, and Freighter services. Jump Freighter contracts support up to 50 Billion ISK collateral. High-value contracts exceeding standard thresholds are routed to Risako Hirano for manual quote review.

      Which EVE Online regions does Vagrant Logistics serve?

      We primarily operate across Domain and Providence, connecting the Amarr imperial trade hub with regional citadels, along with high-volume routes to Jita, Dodixie, Rens, and Hek.

      What ship classes are available for hauling?

      We operate Blockade Runners (up to 12,500 m³), Deep Space Transports (up to 62,500 m³), Freighters & Bowheads (up to 1,125,000 m³), and Jump Freighters (up to 360,000 m³) for dangerous lowsec/nullsec routes.

      How are rewards calculated?

      Quotes combine a base fee, per-jump distance rates (differentiated by Highsec vs Dangerous security status), and a graduated collateral risk surcharge for contracts over 1 Billion ISK.

      Which high-risk systems are automatically avoided?

      All automated route calculations enforce avoidance of Zarzakh, Ahbazon, Rancer, Hagilur, Siseide, Tama, and Aunenen to ensure cargo security.

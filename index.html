      Frequently Asked Questions

      What are the contract duration and expiration requirements?

      Standard stargate courier contracts require 3 Days to Accept and 3 Days to Complete. When opting into a Thera wormhole shortcut, contracts must strictly specify 1 Day to Accept and 1 Day to Complete to guarantee delivery before wormhole connections collapse (Thera Wormhole Mechanics).

      How does Thera wormhole shortcut routing work?

      Thera routing provides optional wormhole shortcuts exclusively for Blockade Runners (cargo volume up to 12,500 m³). It is turned off by default. When an active wormhole route significantly reduces jump distance across New Eden, an interactive "Via Thera" toggle button appears next to the route summary, allowing you to opt into the wormhole path.

      Why must I enter cargo volume before planning a route?

      Automated route planning requires cargo volume because route qualification depends directly on your ship class. Cargo volume determines whether wormhole mass limits permit a Thera shortcut, whether subcapital lowsec stargates can be traversed, and whether highsec-only safe routing must be locked for super-freighters.

      What are the collateral limits for courier contracts?

      Standard subcapital collateral limit is 5 Billion ISK across all Blockade Runner, Deep Space Transport, and Freighter services. Jump Freighter contracts support up to 50 Billion ISK collateral. High-value contracts exceeding standard thresholds are routed to Risako Hirano for manual quote review.

      Which EVE Online regions does Vagrant Logistics serve?

      We primarily operate across Domain and Providence, connecting the Amarr imperial trade hub with regional citadels, along with high-volume routes to Jita, Dodixie, Rens, and Hek (Dotlan Domain / Providence).

      What ship classes are available for hauling?

      We operate Blockade Runners (up to 12,500 m³), Deep Space Transports (up to 62,500 m³), Freighters & Bowheads (up to 1,125,000 m³), and Jump Freighters (up to 360,000 m³) for dangerous lowsec/nullsec routes (EVE University Hauling Guide).

      How are courier contract rewards calculated?

      Quotes combine a transparent base service fee, per-jump distance rates (differentiated by Highsec vs Dangerous space security status), and a graduated collateral risk surcharge for contracts exceeding 1 Billion ISK.

      Which high-risk solar systems are automatically avoided?

      All automated route calculations enforce mandatory avoidance of high-risk choke points and smartbomb camps: Zarzakh, Ahbazon, Rancer, Hagilur, Siseide, Tama, and Aunenen.
